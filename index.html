About AgroLoci 51

Science behind the brand

AGROLOCI 51

Agro represents Agriculture. Loci is the plural of locus, a fundamental genetics term. 51 is a tribute to Photo 51 associated with Rosalind Franklin's X-ray diffraction work that contributed to understanding DNA structure.
